About Us

Together for a Brighter Future

ShivaUmeed Foundation is a registered Section 8 non-profit company under the Companies Act, 2013, committed to creating lasting social impact in the communities that need it most.

Based in Raisen, Madhya Pradesh, we believe that "A better tomorrow begins with a small hope." Our name itself carries this vision — Shiva symbolising strength and auspiciousness, and Umeed meaning hope.

As a Section 8 company, we are empowered to undertake a comprehensive range of charitable, social, and developmental activities — from promoting education and healthcare, to supporting livelihoods, environmental conservation, disaster relief, and community welfare.

What We Do

Our Focus Areas

We work across multiple domains to create holistic and lasting change in communities across India.

Education · शिक्षा

Education & Literacy

Providing quality education and learning opportunities to underprivileged children and adults. From scholarships and school supplies to digital literacy and adult education programmes — education gives direction to dreams.

  • Free tuition & coaching centres
  • School supply distribution
  • Digital literacy workshops
  • Scholarship programmes
Skills · कौशल

Skill Development

Equipping youth and marginalised communities with vocational training and employable skills. We believe skills provide self-reliance, enabling individuals to earn with dignity.

  • Vocational training programs
  • Computer & IT skill courses
  • Entrepreneurship workshops
  • Handicraft & livelihood training
Health · स्वास्थ्य

Healthcare & Wellness

Organising health camps, spreading awareness about preventive healthcare, and ensuring access to basic medical facilities in underserved areas. A healthy community is a thriving community.

  • Free health check-up camps
  • Nutrition awareness drives
  • Mental health support
  • Sanitation & hygiene education
Women · महिला सशक्तिकरण

Women Empowerment

Supporting women through self-help groups, financial literacy, and leadership development. Empowered women build strong families and lay the foundation for a progressive society.

  • Self-Help Group formation
  • Financial literacy programmes
  • Women safety awareness
  • Micro-enterprise support
Rural Dev · ग्रामीण विकास

Rural Development

Building infrastructure, promoting sustainable agriculture, and uplifting rural communities. Strong villages build strong India — and we are committed to making that a reality.

  • Clean water & sanitation projects
  • Agricultural training & support
  • Road & infrastructure development
  • Community centre construction
Environment · पर्यावरण

Environment & Conservation

Protecting our natural heritage through tree plantation drives, waste management awareness, and promoting sustainable living practices in rural and urban communities alike.

  • Tree plantation drives
  • Waste management awareness
  • River & water body cleaning
  • Eco-friendly livelihood practices
