Community Outreach / Humanitarian Activities AIIMS Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh 20 November 2026

A Helping Hand at AIIMS Bhopal: Standing Beside Those Who Need Us Most

At ShivaUmeed Foundation, we believe humanity is not just about offering help; it is about listening, understanding, and standing beside people during their most difficult moments.

With this commitment, our team visited AIIMS Bhopal to interact with patients and vulnerable individuals, understand their challenges, and provide guidance and support to those facing difficult circumstances while seeking medical treatment.

01 · Listening with care

A Story of Courage: A Cancer Patient's Struggle for Treatment and Shelter

During our outreach visit, our team encountered a 51-year-old individual who shared a deeply moving account of his medical and personal struggles.

The individual told our team that he had been undergoing treatment at AIIMS Bhopal continuously for approximately eight months. Despite this prolonged medical journey, he was living on the footpath, facing the additional hardships of finding shelter and managing everyday necessities while seeking treatment.

During our interaction, our team also came across his histopathology report, which provided further insight into his medical condition. According to the report dated 22 October 2026, the examination was conducted on an excised growth from the left lateral side of the tongue. The microscopic examination identified hyperplastic and hyperkeratotic squamous epithelium with moderate dysplasia and superficial sub-epithelial invasion.

The pathologist's final impression described the findings as suggestive of well-differentiated squamous cell carcinoma, a type of oral cancer. The report also mentioned that the specimen consisted of multiple fragmented pieces, making it impossible to assess the surgical resection margins.

A note on medical information

The individual informed our team that he had been told his condition was Stage II cancer. However, the histopathology report itself does not establish the cancer stage. This article does not replace medical advice or a clinician's assessment.

What particularly touched us was the contrast between the medical challenges he was facing and his living conditions. A person undergoing prolonged cancer treatment should not have to face the additional burden of homelessness and uncertainty without access to appropriate support.

Our volunteers listened to his circumstances, discussed his difficulties, and explored possible avenues for guidance and assistance.

02 · Supporting dignity

Extending Support to a Person Living with Partial Paralysis

During the same visit, our team also met an individual living with partial paralysis.

We interacted with him to understand his physical challenges, daily difficulties, and the circumstances affecting his quality of life.

Living with a physical disability can create additional challenges, particularly when access to healthcare, mobility, financial resources, and everyday necessities is limited.

Our team took the time to listen to his concerns and understand his situation, with the intention of identifying possible ways to guide him towards appropriate assistance.

03 · Making time to listen

Listening to the Concerns of Elderly People

Our outreach also included interactions with elderly individuals, including an elderly woman who shared her concerns with our volunteers.

We patiently listened to her experiences, asked about the difficulties she was facing, and tried to understand what kind of support or guidance she might need.

We believe that elderly people, particularly those facing financial or social difficulties, deserve care, respect, and attention.

Sometimes, a conversation, a little guidance, or simply having someone listen can make a meaningful difference in someone's day.

04 · The wider picture

Beyond Medical Treatment: The Need for Humanity and Social Support

Our visit to AIIMS Bhopal reminded us that healthcare challenges extend beyond hospital consultations and medical procedures.

For people experiencing poverty, homelessness, disability, or prolonged illness, everyday survival can become another struggle alongside their medical conditions.

Access to treatment is important, but so are shelter, food, dignity, emotional support, and awareness of available welfare assistance.

At ShivaUmeed Foundation, we aim to help bridge the gap between people in need and the support systems available to them through community outreach, awareness, guidance, and humanitarian initiatives.

We understand that we cannot resolve every difficulty in a single visit. However, we remain committed to listening, understanding, and making sincere efforts to help those who need support.

Because no one should have to face life's most difficult battles feeling forgotten or alone.
